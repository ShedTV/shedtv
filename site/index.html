ShedTV

TV that keeps playing

A better player for the TV you already have. ShedTV is free for Fire TV, Android TV and Android: sign in with the TV service you already use and get a fast guide, catch-up, recording and downloads that cope when your connection doesn't, all set up the way you like.

Install ShedTV On a Fire Stick, type shedtv.app/get in Downloader

What it does

Built around the remote, kind to slow boxes, and stubborn about keeping the picture going.

⌂

Home & watchlist

Add anything you like to your watchlist (a show, an event, a team) and ShedTV picks it out of your guide, with a reminder just before it starts.

▦

Proper TV guide

A fast grid guide with categories, favourites, a muted preview, and one entry per channel whatever quality versions your provider lists.

⟲

Catch-up & rewind

Watch from the start of something already on, go back days on channels that keep an archive, or pause and rewind live TV.

●

Recording that keeps going

If a stream glitches, ShedTV reconnects, tries another version or a backup subscription, and carries on instead of giving up.

⬇

Downloads

Save films and series to watch offline, on the box, a USB drive or your phone. With no internet it opens straight to them.

🔁

Works with your service

Xtream logins or M3U playlists, several subscriptions with automatic fallback, subtitles, search across everything, and updates from inside the app.

Make it yours

Everyone watches differently, so almost everything can be changed. Every setting explains what it does and whether it affects speed or storage.

Install

ShedTV isn't in the app stores yet, so it's installed directly. It takes a couple of minutes, and after that it updates itself.

  1. Install Downloader. From the Fire TV home screen, search for Downloader (orange icon, by AFTVnews) and install it.
  2. Turn on developer options. Go to Settings → My Fire TV → About, then press OK on your device name seven times until it says you're a developer.
  3. Allow Downloader to install apps. Go back to My Fire TV → Developer options → Install unknown apps and switch Downloader on. On older Fire TVs (such as the original 2018 Fire TV Stick 4K) it's a single switch called Apps from Unknown Sources: turn it on.
  4. Get ShedTV. Open Downloader, type shedtv.app/get and press Go. When it's downloaded, choose Install.
  5. Done. Open ShedTV from your apps, add your subscription, and set it up the way you like.
  1. Install Downloader. Open the Play Store on your TV, search for Downloader by AFTVnews and install it.
  2. Turn on developer options. Go to Settings → System → About, then press OK on Android TV OS build seven times.
  3. Allow Downloader to install apps. In Settings → Apps → Security & restrictions → Unknown sources (the name varies slightly by device), switch Downloader on.
  4. Get ShedTV. Open Downloader, type shedtv.app/get and choose Install when it's downloaded.
  5. No ☰ button on your remote? No problem: hold OK anywhere you'd press ☰ to get the same options.
  1. Download. On your phone, open shedtv.app/get in your browser.
  2. Allow the install. When Android asks, allow your browser to install apps, then tap Install.
  3. Done. ShedTV works on phones and tablets too, and is handy for downloading films for a journey.
Why "unknown apps"? Android asks this for any app that doesn't come from an app store. ShedTV is signed with its own key, and in-app updates only install if they're signed with that same key.

Questions

Does ShedTV come with channels?

No. ShedTV is a player only: it doesn't provide, sell or link to any channels or content. You sign in with a TV service you're entitled to use.

What kind of subscription does it work with?

Services that offer an Xtream Codes login (a server address, username and password), or an M3U playlist address.

Can I try it without a subscription?

Yes. When adding a subscription, choose M3U playlist then Use the ShedTV demo playlist. It contains freely shareable open films and standard test streams, so you can try the player, guide layout and controls. The playlist address is shedtv.app/demo.m3u.

Is it free?

Yes, with no adverts and no accounts.

Which devices are best?

ShedTV works on Android 7.1 and newer: Fire TV devices on Fire OS 6, 7 or 8 (including the original 2018 Fire TV Stick 4K and every 4K Max), plus Google TV, Shield and other Android TV boxes. The newest Fire TV models running Vega OS can't install apps from outside Amazon's store, so ShedTV can't run on those. It runs well on a Fire TV Stick 4K Max. For recording and downloads, a Shield or Android TV box with a USB drive is better, because Fire Sticks have very little storage.

How do updates work?

Settings → App update checks for a new version and installs it in place, keeping your subscriptions, favourites and teams.

ShedTV is a media player. It does not include, host or provide access to any TV channels or content. Use it only with services you're entitled to use.
